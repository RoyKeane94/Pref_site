Holding ourselves accountable

We hold ourselves accountable for our impact and hold others accountable for theirs, too.

Prefequity integrates responsible investment principles into how we operate, measure progress, and report to our investors.

  • We are a signatory to the UN Principles for Responsible Investment

  • We measure and monitor our ESG-related activities

  • We regularly update investors on our ESG efforts

Every investment is assessed against our ESG policy before we commit capital, and monitored throughout our partnership.

  • We vet them for any potential adverse ESG impact

  • We encourage sustainable business practices

  • We monitor ESG metrics in line with our ESG policy
