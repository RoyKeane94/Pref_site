What we collect

Personal data is information that relates to you. This may include your name, postal address, email address, phone number, company name, job title and business affiliations. How we use it

We may use your information to contact you in the ordinary course of our business, including sending marketing information. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

You may ask us to stop using your data for marketing, or to delete it, by emailing {{ office_email }}. We will not delete data where we need to keep it for an existing business relationship, or for legal or regulatory reasons.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to keep data accurate and to protect it from unauthorised access, alteration or loss. We review the data we hold regularly and destroy it when it is no longer needed, unless we must keep it for compliance, legal or regulatory purposes.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected, to restrict its processing, or to ask us to delete it. You may also ask for a copy in a machine-readable format. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you are not satisfied with how we handle your data.

Email

Emails from a Prefequity address are intended only for the named recipient and may be confidential. If you are not the intended recipient, please notify the sender, delete the message, and do not copy or share it. Internet communications cannot be guaranteed as secure or error-free.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
